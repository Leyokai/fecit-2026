MISSION 1 — THE ROYAL COUNCIL

10 XP • GRAMMAR GEAR

Quest: Lord Chronos corrupted the royal records. Restore the sentences using the Simple Present!

⚔️ Challenge 1 — Restore the Royal Records

Complete the sentences with the correct form of the verbs.

  1. I English every day. (study)
  2. She soccer after school. (play)
  3. They video games at night. (play)
  4. He his homework in the afternoon. (do)
  5. We breakfast at 7 o’clock. (eat)

🛡️ Challenge 2 — Defeat the Grammar Curse

  1. He play basketball.
  2. She study English.
  3. They plays video games.

MISSION 2 — THE ROYAL TRAINING GROUNDS

10 XP • FREQUENCY GEAR

Quest: The knights forgot their habits. Rebuild their daily routines to recover the second fragment!

🏹 Challenge 1 — The Knights’ Habits

Complete with the correct adverb of frequency.

always • usually • often • sometimes • rarely • never
  1. Sir Arthur trains every day. He trains.
  2. He eats pizza from time to time. He eats pizza.
  3. The royal wizard doesn’t drink coffee. He drinks coffee.
  4. The knights play soccer on many days. They play soccer.
  5. The king goes to the cinema once or twice a year. He goes to the cinema.

🧩 Challenge 2 — Rebuild the Daily Routine

Organize the words.

  1. always / I / study / English
  2. plays / usually / she / volleyball
  3. never / he / eats / fish
  4. sometimes / we / watch / movies

✨ Challenge 2 — The Magical Positioning

Choose the correct alternative.

  1. She ______ studies English.
    □ a) always    □ b) is always
  2. He ______ late for school.
    □ a) never is    □ b) is never
  3. They ______ play basketball.
    □ a) sometimes    □ b) are sometimes

Magic Grammar Scroll

Simple Present

I / You / We / They

I play soccer.

They study English.

He / She / It

He plays soccer.

She studies English.

Adverbs of Frequency

Always • Usually • Often

Sometimes • Rarely • Never

Before the main verb:

I always study.

After verb to be:

I am always happy.
