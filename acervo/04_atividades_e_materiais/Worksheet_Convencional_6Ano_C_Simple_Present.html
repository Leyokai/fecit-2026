Quadro de Apoio Gramatical — Simple Present (Rotina Diária)
Forma Afirmativa (Affirmative) Forma Negativa (Negative) Forma Interrogativa (Interrogative)
• I / You / We / They + verbo na forma base:
Ex.: I wake up early. / They study English.
• He / She / It + verbo com -s, -es ou -ies:
Ex.: He wakes up early. / She goes to school. 		• I / You / We / They + don't + verbo base:
Ex.: They don't watch TV in the morning.
• He / She / It + doesn't + verbo base (sem -s):
Ex.: He doesn't play soccer at night. 		• Do + I/you/we/they + verbo base?
Ex.: Do they study in the afternoon?
• Does + he/she/it + verbo base (sem -s)?
Ex.: Does she have breakfast at 7:00?

Lucas is a sixth-grade student in Campo Grande. Every weekday, he wakes up at 6:30 in the morning, brushes his teeth, and has breakfast with his family. In the afternoon, he goes to school and studies English and other subjects with his classmates. At night, Lucas and Marina watch TV and go to bed at 9:30.

4. Identificação de Estruturas Gramaticais (Formas Afirmativa, Negativa e Interrogativa)

Assinale a única alternativa gramaticalmente correta em cada item, aplicando as regras do Simple Present:

  1. Para dizer em inglês que "Pedro não vai para a escola de manhã" (sujeito = He), a frase correta na forma negativa é:
    (   ) Pedro don't go to school in the morning.
    (   ) Pedro doesn't goes to school in the morning.
    (   ) Pedro doesn't go to school in the morning.
    (   ) Pedro not go to school in the morning.
  2. Qual alternativa apresenta a forma correta de perguntar "Os alunos estudam inglês na terça-feira?" (sujeito = They)?
    (   ) Does the students study English on Tuesday?
    (   ) Do the students studies English on Tuesday?
    (   ) Are the students study English on Tuesday?
    (   ) Do the students study English on Tuesday?
  3. Complete corretamente a pergunta na 3ª pessoa do singular: "________ Ana ________ up at six o'clock?"
    (   ) Do / wake (   ) Does / wake (   ) Does / wakes (   ) Is / waking
  4. Compare as frases: "He collects the notebooks." e "They collect the notebooks." Qual é a explicação correta para a diferença no verbo?
    (   ) Com He, She, It no Simple Present afirmativo, acrescenta-se -s ao verbo; com I, You, We, They, o verbo permanece na forma base.
    (   ) O verbo recebe -s apenas quando o sujeito está no plural.
    (   ) A primeira frase está no passado e a segunda frase está no presente.
